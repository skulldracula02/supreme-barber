## Project overview
Jeremiah Collections is a Pretoria Central fashion and lifestyle retailer. The reference supplied for this project describes apparel, shoes, watches, bags, caps and fragrances, with a focus on personal, expressive style. The target audience is shoppers looking for considered clothing and lifestyle accessories who want to browse a collection and confirm current availability or visit details.

## Goals and success measures
- Present the approved organisation and its product categories clearly.
- Help visitors browse collections and reach the Pretoria store.
- Make email, phone, address and opening hours easy to find.
- Provide a responsive, readable website with consistent navigation.

After launch, track monthly visits, collection views, clicks to telephone and email, and product enquiries. Establish the first-month baseline and compare monthly. The static enquiry form opens an email application and does not measure submissions; a server-backed form or commerce service would be needed for that.

## Design and user experience
The site follows the supplied visual reference: a spacious white layout, small uppercase navigation, charcoal headings, orange italic emphasis, coral calls to action, a yellow values strip, editorial collection cards and a dark contact footer. Product categories and imagery are organised so visitors can browse apparel, shoes, watches and accessories. Navigation adapts to smaller screens; skip links, semantic headings, descriptive image text and labeled form controls support accessibility.

## Technical plan and file structure
The prototype uses HTML5 and CSS3. Product photography is embedded from Unsplash pages listed below and needs an internet connection. No checkout, live inventory or online booking is implemented. The enquiry form uses a `mailto:` action. A later production version could add a secure contact endpoint and stock-aware commerce platform.
