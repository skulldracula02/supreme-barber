## Website content plan

Home introduces the brand and highlights the collections. Collections groups apparel, shoes, watches and accessories. Our Story presents the supplied positioning. Gallery offers a visual edit. Enquiry prepares an email about a product. Contact shows address, map search, phone, email and opening hours.
