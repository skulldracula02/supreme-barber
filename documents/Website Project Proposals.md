## Proposal A: Supreme Barber

### Organisation overview
Supreme Barber is a small hair and grooming business. Its current service information lists haircuts, hair braiding, weave installation and hairstyling. The website project will make that information easier to find and provide a direct route for questions. The proposed mission is to offer attentive, dependable grooming and styling that helps clients express their personal style. The proposed vision is to become a trusted local destination known for skilled work and welcoming service. The intended audience is existing and prospective clients seeking clear service information and a convenient way to contact the business. The mission, vision and audience description are planning proposals that should be confirmed by the owner.

### Website goals and measures
The site should improve online visibility, explain available services, reinforce a professional identity and make contact details accessible on every page. Success can be measured through monthly visits, views of the services page, clicks on phone and email links, and the number of enquiries received. A baseline should be recorded in the first month after launch and reviewed monthly. ### Features and experience
The proposed six-page structure includes Home, About, Services, Gallery, Enquiry and Contact. Home introduces the business and leads visitors towards service details. About describes the proposed mission and approach. Services lists haircuts, braiding, weave installation and styling without inventing prices. Gallery is reserved for real work photographs shared with permission. Enquiry prepares an email, while Contact displays the supplied phone number and email. A consistent navigation menu, readable contrast, mobile-friendly layout and clear calls to action support straightforward use. The visual direction pairs charcoal, warm gold and cream with a simple sans-serif typeface.
